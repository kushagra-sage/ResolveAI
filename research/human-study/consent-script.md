# Consent script

Participation is voluntary. The study evaluates two support-system responses using synthetic or
approved public cases. The form records the participant's full name, Google-account email, and
assigned participant ID for attendance and duplicate-response control. Name and email must be
removed before research analysis, and results must be reported only in aggregate. Do not enter
phone numbers or real customer information. Participants may stop before submitting. The study
supervisor must confirm institutional approval, access controls, retention, and deletion dates
before data collection.
